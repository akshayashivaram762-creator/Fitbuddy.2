YOUR WEEKLY ROUTINE

📅 7-Day Fitness Plan

A simple weekly routine to help you stay active and consistent.

01

Day 1 – Full Body

Gentle warm-up, squats, wall push-ups and light core movements.

🏋️ Workout
02

Day 2 – Cardio

Brisk walking, easy cycling or another comfortable cardio activity.

🏃 Cardio
03

Day 3 – Mobility

Gentle stretching and controlled mobility movements for the body.

🧘 Mobility
04

Day 4 – Lower Body

Controlled squats, chair sit-to-stand movements and gentle leg exercises.

🦵 Lower Body
05

Day 5 – Upper Body

Wall push-ups and simple controlled upper-body movements.

💪 Upper Body
06

Day 6 – Active Movement

Walking, cycling, dancing or another enjoyable light activity.

⚡ Active Day
07

Day 7 – Recovery

Gentle movement, relaxed stretching and enough rest for recovery.

💚 Recovery
💡

FitBuddy Tip

Listen to your body, stay hydrated and give yourself enough time to recover.
